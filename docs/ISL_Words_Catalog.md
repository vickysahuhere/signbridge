## 🔤 1. ISL Fingerspelling & Alphabets (A–Z)

| Letter | Type | Category | Quick Tip | How to Sign / Movement |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Vowel | *Vowel #1: Non-dominant thumb tip* | Touch the tip of your non-dominant thumb with your dominant index finger. (Or single-handed: closed fist with thumb upright along the side). |
| **B** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Circles joined or 4 fingers up* | Both hands form circles with thumb and index touching, joined together like binoculars (or 4 straight fingers upright with thumb folded). |
| **C** | Single-Handed | Consonant | *Curved open arc* | Hand curved into a clear, open "C" shape facing inward. |
| **D** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Form a D against vertical index* | Non-dominant index pointing vertically up; dominant thumb and index form a curved semicircle touching its tip and base to form a "D". |
| **E** | Two-Handed ISL | Vowel | *Vowel #2: Non-dominant index tip* | Touch the tip of your non-dominant index finger with your dominant index finger. |
| **F** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Two fingers crossed horizontally* | Dominant index and middle fingers placed horizontally across non-dominant index and middle fingers. |
| **G** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Fists stacked or horizontal index* | Dominant fist placed on top of or against non-dominant fist (or index and thumb pointing horizontally). |
| **H** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Flat hand sweeping across palm* | Dominant flat open palm placed flat across non-dominant open palm. |
| **I** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Vowel | *Vowel #3: Middle tip / Pinky up* | Touch the tip of your non-dominant middle finger with your dominant index finger. (Or single-handed: pinky extended straight up from fist). |
| **J** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Trace down palm from middle finger* | Dominant index touches non-dominant middle fingertip and draws a "J" curve down the palm to the wrist. |
| **K** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Hooked finger on index joint* | Dominant index finger hooked/bent touching the first knuckle/joint of non-dominant extended index finger. |
| **L** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Vertical index on flat palm / L-shape* | Dominant extended index finger resting vertically on non-dominant flat palm (forming an "L"). Or thumb and index at 90° angle. |
| **M** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *3 fingers resting on palm* | Dominant 3 fingers (index, middle, ring) resting downward onto non-dominant flat palm. |
| **N** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *2 fingers resting on palm* | Dominant 2 fingers (index, middle) resting downward onto non-dominant flat palm. |
| **O** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Vowel | *Vowel #4: Ring tip / O-circle* | Touch the tip of your non-dominant ring finger with your dominant index finger. (Or single-handed: all fingertips touching thumb tip forming an O). |
| **P** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Circle loop touching index tip* | Dominant thumb and index form a circle loop touching the tip of non-dominant index finger pointing vertically up. |
| **Q** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Circle hooked onto thumb* | Dominant thumb and index form a circle hooked around the base of non-dominant extended thumb. |
| **R** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Hooked finger on palm* | Dominant hooked/curved index finger resting on the center of non-dominant flat palm. |
| **S** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Pinkies linked or fist on palm* | Dominant pinky finger hooked with non-dominant pinky finger, or dominant closed fist resting against non-dominant palm. |
| **T** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Index touching side edge of palm* | Dominant extended index finger touching the side edge of non-dominant flat palm near the pinky base. |
| **U** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Vowel | *Vowel #5: Pinky tip* | Touch the tip of your non-dominant pinky finger with your dominant index finger. (Or single-handed: index and middle extended together). |
| **V** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *V on palm / Peace sign* | Dominant index and middle fingers spread in a "V" resting on non-dominant open palm (or single-handed peace sign). |
| **W** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Interlaced fingers / 3 fingers up* | Both hands with open palms, fingers interlaced together (or single-handed 3 fingers spread up). |
| **X** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Crossed index fingers* | Both index fingers crossed over each other to form an "X". |
| **Y** | Two-Handed ISL / 1-Hand | Consonant | *Index in thumb-index crotch* | Dominant index finger placed into the V-crotch between non-dominant extended thumb and index finger (or thumb and pinky extended). |
| **Z** | Two-Handed ISL | Consonant | *Flat hand on hand edge* | Dominant flat hand resting along the upper edge of non-dominant flat hand. |
